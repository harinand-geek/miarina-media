01

Brand

Flow mark. A play triangle preceded by three motion bars that read as media streaming in. Built on a 32-unit grid, corner radius 8/32. Minimum size 16 px. Clear space equals one bar length (4 units) on all sides.
Miarina Media
On light grounds the mark inverts to ink tile with teal glyph. Wordmark: Manrope ExtraBold, tracking −2%.
