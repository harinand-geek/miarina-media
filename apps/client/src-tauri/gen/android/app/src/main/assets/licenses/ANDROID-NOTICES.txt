Includes Python, yt-dlp, QuickJS and FFmpeg; each retains its upstream license.
Build recipes and upstream sources are referenced in that release's
BUILD_PYTHON.md and BUILD_FFMPEG.md. Do not substitute different native builds
without reviewing their licenses and corresponding sources.

No in-app download of extractor updates is enabled for this integration.
Runtime updates are delivered with a new signed APK.

The application replaces the library's older yt-dlp script with yt-dlp
2026.08.19, packaged as res/raw/ytdlp (standalone Python archive).
