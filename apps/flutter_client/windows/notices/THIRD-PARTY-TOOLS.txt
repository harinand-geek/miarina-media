Miarina Media - third-party components shipped with the application
==================================================================

Miarina Media runs yt-dlp, FFmpeg and QuickJS-NG as separate processes
(this folder); its player loads libmpv (libmpv-2.dll, next to
miarina_media.exe). None of them is modified. Each file was taken from the
official release below and checked against a pinned SHA-256 digest before
being packaged.
